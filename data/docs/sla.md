# Service Level Agreement

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") applies to the production Service provided by Brightline Data, Inc. ("Provider") under the Master Services Agreement.

## 1. Uptime Commitment

Provider will make the Service available at least 99.9% of the time in each calendar month ("Monthly Uptime"). Monthly Uptime is calculated as the total minutes in the month minus Downtime minutes, divided by the total minutes in the month.

## 2. Service Credits

If Monthly Uptime falls below the commitment, Customer is eligible for a service credit as a percentage of that month's fees: 10% if Monthly Uptime is below 99.9% but at least 99.0%; 25% if it is below 99.0% but at least 95.0%; and 50% if it is below 95.0%. Service credits are applied to future invoices and are never paid out as cash.

## 3. Exclusions

Downtime does not include unavailability caused by scheduled maintenance announced at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance, factors outside Provider's reasonable control such as internet outages or force majeure events, Customer's equipment or third-party software, or suspension under the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 4. Claiming Credits

To receive a service credit, Customer must submit a claim to support within thirty (30) days after the end of the month in which the Downtime occurred, including the dates and times of the Downtime. Service credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet the uptime commitment.

## 5. Support Response Times

Provider will respond to support tickets within these targets: Severity 1 (Service unavailable) within one (1) hour, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week; Severity 2 (major feature impaired) within four (4) business hours; and Severity 3 (general questions) within one (1) business day.
