# Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs Customer's use of the Service provided by Brightline Data, Inc. ("Provider").

## 1. Prohibited Uses

Customer must not use the Service to: send unsolicited bulk messages; upload malware; attempt to probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service without Provider's prior written consent; store payment card numbers in fields not designated for them; or resell the Service without a written reseller agreement.

## 2. API Rate Limits

Each Customer account may make up to 1,000 API requests per minute. Requests above the limit receive an HTTP 429 response. Customer may request a higher limit through support, and Provider will not unreasonably refuse a request for an account on an annual plan.

## 3. Suspension

Provider may suspend Customer's access immediately, without prior notice, if Customer's use poses a security risk to the Service or to other customers or violates applicable law. For any other violation of this AUP, Provider will give Customer written notice and five (5) business days to cure before suspending. Provider will restore access promptly once the issue is resolved.

## 4. Reporting Violations

Anyone may report a suspected violation of this AUP to abuse@brightline.example. Provider will acknowledge reports within two (2) business days.
